HOW TO WIN THE COUCH

01 · RAM SMARTHard hits drain hull. A nose-first slam into their side or rear hurts far more than a gentle head-on tap.
02 · RING-OUTShove them past the stitched edge and they tumble off the cushion. Instant round win, whatever the hull bars say.
03 · TURBOHold turbo to burn the blue meter. Tap it for a dash: extra speed plus a damage bonus on impact. Meter refills slowly.
04 · SPARK PLUGSGold pickups appear on the cushion. Drive over one for +45 turbo and +8 hull.
05 · SUDDEN DEATHAt 0:00 the cushion starts shrinking and both cars take damage. Someone always loses.
06 · BEST OF 3First to two round wins takes the couch. Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts the match.
07 · TWO PHONESPick TWO PHONES, create a room, then tap INVITE to share the link (or send the 4-letter code). Your friend opens it on their phone. If someone drops out, the match freezes and resumes on reconnect.
